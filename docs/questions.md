# Questions and suggestions

The admin page has a **Vragen** tab, with a counter on the tab. The system asks there about what it does not know yet, and makes suggestions. A generator runs every 10 minutes. Every question has a key that is used only once, so nothing is asked twice, and only a few questions of each kind are open at a time.

| Kind | When | Answer | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Persoon ("Wie is dit?") | Highlight of the last 7 days with a person, no face recognised by Frigate, judged a real event by the AI, not labelled yet. Best first; at most 3 open. In 0.13–0.17 only when there was something to learn (a face crop or `person_marker`); since 0.18.0 always, so the answer can put a name on the highlight | Bewoner / bezoeker / bezorger of dienst / onbekend, optional name (suggestions from Frigate's face library; prefilled with Frigate's best guess of at least 50 %). Since 0.13.0 there is **one question per highlight**: a highlight that already had a question (of any kind, answered or not) gets no other. Open questions that no longer apply are withdrawn by the system (shown as dismissed by "systeem"): a person question whose face crop Frigate no longer keeps and without another stable feature, Questions about a highlight that is deleted are closed automatically. Handled questions stay visible as history, with the fact and the face-learning result.

The vehicle question needs two extra AI fields (since 0.10.0): `vehicle`, an exact description of a vehicle (motor vehicles only in 0.13.x; bicycles and scooters again since 0.14.0) or empty, and `vehicle_known`, whether it matches a fact. The person question uses `person_marker` (since 0.13.0).

## What an answer changes (since 0.13.0)

1. **Label.** The answer is stored on the highlight ("Volgens beheer: …" in the archive). On the TV it appears after the camera name, except "onbekend" answers.
2. **New description.** With AI enabled, the highlight is described again, with the answer in the prompt as reliable information, so the TV shows e.g. "Tineke loopt naar de container" instead of "Een persoon loopt …". This also happens for older highlights and for highlights that dropped out of the selection. The old description stays until the new one is there.
3. **Counts low.** A named resident or visitor, and a vehicle with an owner, make the highlight count as known (× 0.4, like a face Frigate recognised; see [highlights.md](highlights.md)). Highlights with such an answer stay in the archive even when they fall below the threshold. Couriers (`bezorger`) and "onbekend" do not count as known.
4. **Fact.** As before: vehicle answers directly, person answers through the fact writer.
5. **Face learning.** For a named *bewoner* or *bezoeker*, the unrecognised face crops Frigate kept for the highlight's detections (at most 10, best guess first) are moved into Frigate's face library under that name, through `POST /api/faces/train/<name>/classify`. A typed name is matched to an existing library name (case-insensitive, or a unique first name: "tineke" → "Tineke Mevius"); otherwise Frigate creates a new person. Frigate then recognises that person in later alerts. Couriers' faces are never learned (different people, same company). Since 0.20.0 Beheer → Gezichten shows the library per person (newest first); an administrator selects photos of somebody else, or too blurry ones, and removes them (`POST /api/faces/<name>/delete`, only files of that person). Frigate trusts every library photo completely, so one wrong photo makes it mix people up. A worker handles this right after the answer and every 5 minutes; five failed attempts mark it failed. This needs the Frigate account `uitkijk` to have the admin role (see [security.md](security.md)).

Frigate keeps about the last 100 unrecognised face crops (`save_attempts`), roughly two weeks at the 2026-11 rate of about 7 a day. A crop is only there when Frigate found a face of at least `min_area` (500 px²) in the 640×360 detection stream, so people seen from behind, far away or at night usually have none.

**Faces from the recording (since 0.19.0).** When Frigate kept no crop, the face worker looks at the 4K recording instead: for each person in the highlight's detections who stood at least a quarter of the frame tall and was alone on that camera at the time, up to 8 frames around where Frigate saw them best are fetched (`/api/<camera>/recordings/<time>/snapshot.jpg`) and cropped to the person. Each crop goes to `POST /api/faces/recognize`. Crops without a face are dropped. Of the rest, at most 3 are stored through `POST /api/faces/<name>/register`; Frigate finds the face in the crop itself. Faces Frigate is unsure about or puts the wrong name on go first, because they teach it most. On 2026-11-09 Frigate saw none of the faces in the 640×360 stream, while the recording showed them clearly; on the back camera it took Tineke for Johan, Mark and Jasper at 0.97. The handled-questions list shows "N gezichtsfoto's uit de opname toegevoegd aan <naam>". When Frigate cannot process the frames at that moment (HTTP 500, for example while its detector restarts) and no face was found, the attempt counts as a failure and is retried, up to five times. Named answers that found no face before 0.19.1 get this attempt once at startup.

Answers given before 0.11.0/0.13.0 go through these steps once at startup.

## Who is who (since 0.14.0)

Names typed in an answer about a person or a vehicle owner are matched against Frigate's face library (exact, or a unique first name: "jasper" → "Jasper Mevius"), so one person has one spelling in faces, facts and labels. The questions tab shows the library names as first-name buttons. The AI uses first names only in descriptions; when a vehicle matches a fact it names the owner ("Jasper fietst weg", "De auto van Mark rijdt de oprit op"), unless face recognition shows someone else.

## Whose vehicle (since 0.18.0)

The AI lists every vehicle clearly in view (`vehicles`, at most four: cars, vans, motorbikes, scooters, bicycles, also parked ones), each with whether it matches a fact. Every unknown one becomes a "Van wie is deze …?" question, one per distinct description, at most 3 open; a highlight never has two open questions, so a vehicle question can follow once the person question on the same highlight is answered. At the first start of 0.18.0 the selected highlights of the last two days are described once more to fill in their vehicles.
