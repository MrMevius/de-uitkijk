# Questions and suggestions

The admin page has a **Vragen** tab, with a counter on the tab. The system asks there about what it does not know yet, and makes suggestions. A generator runs every 10 minutes. Every question has a key that is used only once, so nothing is asked twice, and only a few questions of each kind are open at a time.

| Kind | When | Answer | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Persoon ("Wie is dit?") | Highlight of the last 7 days with a person, no face recognised by Frigate, judged a real event by the AI, not labelled yet. Best first; at most 3 open. In 0.13–0.17 only when there was something to learn (a face crop or `person_marker`); since 0.18.0 always, so the answer can put a name on the highlight | Bewoner / familie / bezoeker / buren / vaste hulp / bezorger of dienst / voorbijganger / onbekend (familie, buren, vaste hulp and voorbijganger since 0.21.0), optional name (suggestions from Frigate's face library; prefilled with Frigate's best guess of at least 50 %). The face crops are shown | Label on the highlight; the AI describes it again; the AI writes a fact from the still and the answer, if something visible and stable fits (see [ai.md](ai.md#household-facts-since-080-automatic-since-0110)); with a name and a person who comes back (*bewoner*, *familie*, *bezoeker*, *buren* or *vaste hulp*): the face crops go into Frigate's face library and the highlight counts as known |
| Voertuig ("Van wie is deze auto/fiets/scooter/motor?") | The AI described a vehicle in a highlight that matches no fact (since 0.30.0: nothing in the register or the facts): a car (colour, make, model, plate if legible) or, since 0.14.0, a bicycle or scooter (type, colour, brand, fixed parts such as a child seat or panniers). A description of one word ("fiets"), or one in which the AI says it cannot tell the vehicle apart ("merk en model niet duidelijk herkenbaar", since 0.18.1), is too vague and is not asked; steps and skateboards never are, nor (since 0.22.2) wheelie bins, wheelbarrows and handcarts: on 2026-11-11 the AI listed two *rolcontainers* as vehicles and asked "Van wie is deze auto?"; such an open question is withdrawn | Owner (one-click first names from Frigate's face library), with an editable description; or "onbekende auto/fiets" | New AI fact "<description> is van <owner>"; the AI describes the highlight again. Since 0.13.0 there is **one question per highlight**: a highlight that already had a question (of any kind, answered or not) gets no other. Open questions that no longer apply are withdrawn by the system (shown as dismissed by "systeem"), see *Fewer, better questions*. Questions about a highlight that is deleted are closed automatically. Handled questions stay visible as history, with the fact and the face-learning result.

The vehicle question needs two extra AI fields (since 0.10.0): `vehicle`, an exact description of a vehicle (motor vehicles only in 0.13.x; bicycles and scooters again since 0.14.0) or empty, and `vehicle_known`, whether it matches a fact. The person question uses `person_marker` (since 0.13.0).

## What an answer changes (since 0.13.0)

1. **Label.** The answer is stored on the highlight ("Volgens beheer: …" in the archive). On the TV it appears after the camera name, except "onbekend" answers.
2. **New description.** With AI enabled, the highlight is described again, with the answer in the prompt as reliable information, so the TV shows e.g. "Tineke loopt naar de container" instead of "Een persoon loopt …". This also happens for older highlights and for highlights that dropped out of the selection. The old description stays until the new one is there.
3. **Counts low.** A named person who comes back (resident, family, visitor, neighbour or regular help such as a cleaner or gardener), and a vehicle with an owner, make the highlight count as known (× 0.4, like a face Frigate recognised; see [highlights.md](highlights.md)). Highlights with such an answer stay in the archive even when they fall below the threshold. Couriers (`bezorger`), passers-by (`voorbijganger`) and "onbekend" do not count as known; "voorbijganger" and "onbekend" without a name write no fact either.
4. **Fact.** As before: vehicle answers directly, person answers through the fact writer.
5. **Face learning.** For a named *bewoner*, *familie*, *bezoeker*, *buren* or *vaste hulp*, the unrecognised face crops Frigate kept for the highlight's detections (at most 10, best guess first) are moved into Frigate's face library under that name, through `POST /api/faces/train/<name>/classify`. A typed name is matched to an existing library name (case-insensitive, or a unique first name: "tineke" → "Tineke Mevius"); otherwise Frigate creates a new person. Frigate then recognises that person in later alerts. Couriers' faces are never learned (different people, same company), nor are passers-by's. Since 0.20.0 Beheer → Gezichten shows the library per person (newest first); an administrator selects photos of somebody else, or too blurry ones, and removes them (`POST /api/faces/<name>/delete`, only files of that person). Frigate trusts every library photo completely, so one wrong photo makes it mix people up. Frigate rebuilds its face model in the background after each change; several deletions in a row could leave a removed person in it (seen 2026-11-09, until a restart). Since 0.20.2 De Uitkijk asks Frigate once more, 20 s after the last deletion, to rebuild it (a delete without files only clears the model). The 2026-11-09 clean-up removed 69 of 184 photos (blurry, infrared blobs, side views, one of the wrong person); the library before it is kept on VM502 in `/srv/container-runtime/application-state/frigate/config/faces-backup-20261109/`. A worker handles this right after the answer and every 5 minutes; five failed attempts mark it failed. This needs the Frigate account `uitkijk` to have the admin role (see [security.md](security.md)).

Frigate keeps about the last 100 unrecognised face crops (`save_attempts`), roughly two weeks at the 2026-11 rate of about 7 a day. A crop is only there when Frigate found a face of at least `min_area` (500 px²) in the 640×360 detection stream, so people seen from behind, far away or at night usually have none.

**Faces from the recording (since 0.19.0).** When Frigate kept no crop, the face worker looks at the 4K recording instead: for each person in the highlight's detections who stood at least a quarter of the frame tall and was alone on that camera at the time, up to 8 frames around where Frigate saw them best are fetched (`/api/<camera>/recordings/<time>/snapshot.jpg`) and cropped to the person. Each crop goes to `POST /api/faces/recognize`. Crops without a face are dropped. Of the rest, at most 3 are stored through `POST /api/faces/<name>/register`; Frigate finds the face in the crop itself. Faces Frigate is unsure about or puts the wrong name on go first, because they teach it most. Since 0.20.2 every stored crop is checked straight away: narrower than 0.68 (width/height, a side view) or under 40 px wide, it is removed again and the next frame gets a turn (Arjan's first three crops of 2026-11-09 were a forehead, a blurry side view and an ear). On 2026-11-09 Frigate saw none of the faces in the 640×360 stream, while the recording showed them clearly; on the back camera it took Tineke for Johan, Mark and Jasper at 0.97. The handled-questions list shows "N gezichtsfoto's uit de opname toegevoegd aan <naam>". When Frigate cannot process the frames at that moment (HTTP 500, for example while its detector restarts) and no face was found, the attempt counts as a failure and is retried, up to five times. Named answers that found no face before 0.19.1 get this attempt once at startup.

Answers given before 0.11.0/0.13.0 go through these steps once at startup.

## Who is who (since 0.14.0)

Names typed in an answer about a person or a vehicle owner are matched against Frigate's face library (exact, or a unique first name: "jasper" → "Jasper Mevius"), so one person has one spelling in faces, facts and labels. The questions tab shows the library names as first-name buttons. Since 0.22.5 it also shows people named in earlier answers whose face Frigate could not learn (a person who comes back, or a vehicle owner), after the library names and alphabetically; typed names are matched against them in the same way ("roel" → "Roel Snippe"). A button shows the full name when two people share a first name. The list is refreshed after every answer. Since 0.24.2 the household register (Beheer → Huishouden) counts too: its people get a button, and their other names (*ook:*) resolve to the full name, so "Tini" becomes "Tineke Mevius" and gets no button of its own. The AI uses first names only in descriptions; when a vehicle matches a fact it names the owner ("Jasper fietst weg", "De auto van Mark rijdt de oprit op"), unless face recognition shows someone else.

## Whose vehicle (since 0.18.0)

The AI lists every vehicle clearly in view (`vehicles`, at most four: cars, vans, motorbikes, scooters, bicycles, also parked ones), each with whether it matches a fact. Every unknown one becomes a "Van wie is deze …?" question, one per distinct description, at most 3 open; a highlight never has two open questions, so a vehicle question can follow once the person question on the same highlight is answered. At the first start of 0.18.0 the selected highlights of the last two days are described once more to fill in their vehicles.

**Judged against older facts (since 0.22.1).** The AI decides whether a vehicle matches a fact when it describes the highlight, with the facts of that moment. A highlight described before the newest fact is therefore described again before its vehicle question is asked; if the vehicle is still unknown, the question follows in a later run (10 minutes), and a highlight waiting for a new description gets no vehicle question. Before, answering "Witte Hyundai bestelbus is van Roel" freed a place in the queue that was filled eight minutes later with "Van wie is deze auto?" about the same van with a trailer of ladders, described two hours before the answer. This costs at most one AI call per question slot; without AI the question is asked straight away. Since 0.31.0 a removed fact or register entry (retired by a doubt, deleted, merged) counts as a change too.

**Only about highlights (since 0.31.0).** A vehicle question is asked only about a highlight or an event an administrator answered about, the ones in the archive. On 2026-11-13, 14 vehicle questions were about events that had dropped out of the selection after they were described. Such open questions are withdrawn; the same vehicle comes back in a later highlight.

## Fewer, better questions (since 0.27.0)

Up to 2026-11-11, 17 of 43 vehicle questions and 6 of 54 person questions were skipped. What changed:

- **Vehicles out of view.** "deels/grotendeels verborgen", "achter de …" and similar descriptions are not asked: there is nothing to recognise the vehicle by next time.
- **Cars without make or plate.** "donkere sedan", "grijze hatchback" look like many others and are not asked. A plate in the description or a real plate from Frigate's LPR (with a digit: Frigate once read "SCHUUR" from the shed) makes them askable, and so do vans, campers, trailers and vehicles with lettering. Trade-off: some of these were answered before (6 times); those answers gave weak facts.
- **Already told about.** A vehicle is not asked when its plate is one character from a plate in a fact (the AI read G-029-VD for G-929-VD) or its make and model equal those of a fact ("zwarte Volkswagen up!" after "Donkere Volkswagen up! is van Noëlle"). Colour is ignored: the AI calls the same car black, dark or dark grey depending on the light. A make alone is not enough.
- **Tiny people.** The importer stores the height of the largest person box on the clip's camera (`person_size`, a fraction of the frame; 0 = no person on that camera). Below 4% no person question is asked. The limit is low on purpose: the owner named neighbours at 5% of the frame height. The street passers-by at Voordeur that were skipped (10–13%) are handled by the tighter Voordeur zone in Frigate (2026-11-11). Clearly visible people (≥ 30%) are asked first, then by relevance. At the first start of 0.27.0 the highlights of the last week are measured once from Frigate's detections; without them the size stays unknown and the highlight is treated as before.
- Open questions that the new rules would not ask are withdrawn ("systeem").

## Same visit (since 0.27.0)

A person question lists the other highlights within 15 minutes (any camera) with a person nobody named, that Frigate did not recognise, that are not noise and not too small, at most six, closest first. They are ticked; the answer applies to every ticked one: it gets its own answered question (`person:<id>`, so its face is learned too), its label and a new description. Only the first answer becomes a fact, so the same fact is not written six times. Highlights with an earlier answer are never changed. Since 0.28.2 a click on a thumbnail plays that clip large under the list; the tick box is separate.

## Rating from the questions tab (since 0.27.0)

Every question with a highlight has 👍/👎, like the archive: rating while answering costs one tap. Since 0.28.1 an answered or skipped card stays in place with the answer and the 👍/👎 buttons until *Sluiten* or until the tab is left; before, it disappeared at once and the highlight could no longer be rated there.
